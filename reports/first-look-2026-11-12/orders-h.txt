Through Oct 18 there are 45 refunded orders out of 1,342 real ones (3.4%), worth $4,039 in total.

Marketplace is the main source:
- 23 of the 45 refunds, and $2,006 of the money
- 5.6% of its orders get refunded, versus 2.4% on Web and 1.9% on Wholesale

By region, West is highest at 4.6% and South lowest at 1.5%. North and East sit around 3.7% and 3.5%.

Returning customers refund slightly more than new ones, 3.6% versus 3.0%, but the gap is small.

For context, the typical order is about $81 with 3 items.
