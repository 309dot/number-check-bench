We got 317 signups last week (Oct 12-18), not counting 4 internal test accounts. 137 of them finished setup, so overall activation was 43%.

Activation climbs with plan size:
- Free: 37% (74 of 199)
- Starter: 48% (40 of 84)
- Team: 68% (23 of 34)

Top sources:
- Organic search: 98 signups, 31% of the total
- Paid search: 81, 26%
- Direct: 60

Paid search is the only paid channel in the export, so roughly a quarter of signups came from paid.

By country, the US is the biggest at 141 signups (44%), followed by the UK with 46 and France with 33. Germany had 29, while South Korea and Canada each had 23 and Australia 22.
