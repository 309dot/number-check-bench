Here is the hiring pipeline update from the October 18 export.

We have 751 candidates in the pipeline. Most are no longer active: 532 were rejected, 102 withdrew and 34 were hired. That leaves 83 still in play: 32 at Applied, 27 at Screen, 20 at Interview and 4 at Offer.

Offers accepted stand at 34, with 10 more marked as not accepted.

LinkedIn is our biggest source with 281 candidates, followed by the careers page with 219 and referrals with 105.

Backend Engineer has the most candidates at 197, then Support Specialist at 170 and Account Executive at 158.

Applications are steady to slightly up. We received 67 in the week ending October 18, against 64 the week before, a gain of 3 or about 5%.
