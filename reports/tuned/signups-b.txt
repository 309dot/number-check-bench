We got 317 signups last week (Oct 12-18), after removing 4 internal test accounts from the export.

Activation was 43% overall, meaning those people finished setup. It rises with plan size:
- Free: 37%
- Starter: 48%
- Team: 68%

Organic search was our biggest source with 98 signups, followed by paid search with 81 and direct with 60. Paid search was about 26% of the week, so roughly three in four signups came from unpaid channels.

By country, the US led with 45% of signups, then the UK at 15% and France at 10%. The rest was spread across Germany, South Korea, Canada and Australia.

Free signups are the weakest at activating, so that is where I would look first.
