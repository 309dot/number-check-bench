Here is the ad performance for 24 August to 18 October.

We spent 70,762 and generated 1,291 leads, so each lead cost 54.81 on average. Overall click-through rate was 1.06%.

Best and worst campaigns, by cost per lead:
- Best: Harbor & Pine – Brand search at 24.07 per lead.
- Worst: Harbor & Pine – Lookalike at 107.48 per lead.

Search brings in the most leads, 622 of the 1,291, and it is also the cheapest channel per lead.

The last two weeks (5 to 18 October) against the two weeks before (21 September to 4 October):
- Leads rose from 311 to 335.
- Spend rose from 17,041 to 18,733.
- Cost per lead moved from 54.79 to 55.92, so it is slightly worse.

Overall, results are steady, with a gap between the best and worst Harbor & Pine campaigns that is worth a conversation about shifting budget.
