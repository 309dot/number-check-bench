Month to date through October 18, we have 1,342 orders and $131,957 in revenue, an average order value of $98.33. That leaves out 35 cancelled orders and 4 internal test orders.

Refunds: 45 orders were refunded, a refund rate of 3.4%, worth $4,039. The revenue figure above is before those refunds.

Web is our biggest channel at $65,608, or 49.7% of revenue. Marketplace and Wholesale each bring in roughly a quarter. North is the biggest region at $40,398, or 30.6%.

Customers: 770 orders came from returning customers (57.4%) and 572 from new customers (42.6%).

Last week (October 12-18) brought 500 orders and $50,874 in revenue, against 525 orders and $51,483 the week before. Orders are down 4.8% and revenue is down 1.2%, so a slight slowdown rather than a drop-off.
