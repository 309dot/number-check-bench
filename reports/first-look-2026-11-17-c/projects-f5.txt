Which projects to pause

The rule I propose: pause new spend on any project that is High risk, past due and over budget. Only these projects meet all three tests:

- CRM migration (Omar, Sales, Red): 19 days overdue.
- Billing revamp (Chris, Finance, Red): 16 days overdue.
- Data warehouse (Priya, Data, Amber): 16 days overdue.

Together their spend is 106.8% of their budget. Their overrun is the entire portfolio overrun. They account for 38.2% of open spend and 27.2% of open budget.

Keep running, but watch:

- Field sales app (Omar, Sales, Red, High risk) has spent 95.4% of its budget, with 32 days to go. It misses the pause rule only because it is not yet overdue, so the owner should bring a re-plan now.
- SSO rollout is 18 days overdue but has spent 98.8% of its budget. Close it out rather than pause it.
- Warehouse robots pilot is High risk, but it is due in 103 days, so it needs a planning check rather than a pause.
