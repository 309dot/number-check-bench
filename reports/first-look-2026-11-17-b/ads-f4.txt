Quarterly Paid Media Audit: Where to Cut and Where to Scale

Summary

I reviewed the daily campaign export for your three accounts covering the full 56 days from August 24 to October 18. I left out the 56 rows flagged as a test campaign (QA - Pixel test), which spent only $49.01 and produced no leads. I found no duplicate rows, so the review covers 672 rows across 12 campaigns.

The accounts spent $70,762.15 and generated 1,291 leads, an average cost per lead (CPL) of $54.81. Overall click-through rate was 1.06% and 2.27% of clicks became leads.

Is performance getting better or worse?

Flat. The first four weeks produced 645 leads at $54.25 CPL. The most recent four weeks produced 646 leads at $55.38 CPL. Nothing is collapsing, but nothing is compounding either. The gains have to come from moving money, not from waiting.

Channel view

- Search: $40.75 CPL, 5.15% click-through, and 5.42% of clicks converting. It took 35.8% of spend but delivered 48.2% of leads.
- Social: $66.20 CPL, 1.74% click-to-lead rate, and the biggest budget line at 43.8% of spend.
- Display: $71.80 CPL and a 1.09% click-to-lead rate. Display is cheap per click but the clicks rarely turn into people who contact you.

Client view

- Brightline Dental: $42.03 CPL on $21,646 of spend, with 4.5% of clicks becoming leads. This is your healthiest account.
- Northfield Bikes: $54.93 CPL and 2.1% click-to-lead. Right around the average.
- Harbor & Pine: $71.28 CPL, the highest of the three, on the largest budget at $28,296. Only 1.5% of its clicks convert.

What to cut

Four campaigns are doing most of the damage. Harbor & Pine Fall collection, Gift guide and Lookalike, plus Northfield Commuter banner, together took $28,826, which is 40.7% of everything, and returned only 309 leads (23.9% of the total).

- Harbor & Pine Lookalike: $107.48 CPL, the worst in the book. It averaged $97.89 of spend a day for 0.9 leads a day, and it barely improved: its CPL was $105.41 in the last four weeks.
- Harbor & Pine Gift guide: $99.99 CPL with a 0.80% click-to-lead rate, the weakest conversion of any campaign. It buys 1,304,572 impressions, but the audience is not buying.
- Harbor & Pine Fall collection: $95.63 CPL overall, and it is getting worse: $90.18 in the first four weeks versus $100.65 in the last four weeks.
- Northfield Commuter banner: $68.20 CPL and only 0.50% click-through.

Display is not erratic, though. Among Display rows, 0.6% of days ended with zero leads, and across all accounts only 7 account-days had zero leads, so results are steady day to day. The problem is price, not randomness.

Recommendation: cut Lookalike and Gift guide entirely, which frees about $12,781 over the period. Cut Fall collection by half and Commuter banner by half until creative is refreshed. Do not cut Harbor & Pine search; see below.

What to scale

- Harbor & Pine Brand search: $24.07 CPL, the cheapest campaign in the account, with 8.0% click-through. It spent only 5.0% of the budget yet produced 11.5% of leads. Brand search volume is limited, so scale it by widening to close variants, not by raising bids blindly.
- Northfield Service booking: $28.01 CPL and 5.7% click-to-lead, on the smallest budget of the twelve campaigns.
- Brightline Emergency care: $39.75 CPL and a 9.1% click-to-lead rate, the best conversion rate in the file, even though its cost per click is $3.60.
- Brightline New patient offer: $39.47 CPL on social, proof that Social can work when the offer is concrete. Its median daily spend is $112.55.

These four campaigns took only 23.7% of spend, and drove 39.3% of all leads. At their current CPL, moving the $12,781 freed from the two worst campaigns into them would buy roughly 330 extra leads, versus the 124 those two delivered. I would assume diminishing returns and plan for about half of that.

Watch list

- Northfield E-bike search: $74.56 CPL at $2.20 per click, and only 2.95% of clicks convert. The traffic is there (4.0% click-through), so I suspect the landing page or price, not the keywords.
- Brightline Implants search: $44.70 CPL, acceptable for a high-value treatment, and the largest Brightline line.
- Brightline Retargeting: $44.71 CPL on display, far better than the Display average, so warm audiences are the only Display worth keeping.

Next steps

1. This week: pause Lookalike and Gift guide, and halve Fall collection and Commuter banner.
2. Move that budget into Brand search, Service booking, Emergency care and New patient offer in small weekly steps, watching CPL after each step.
3. Audit the E-bike search landing page, since its click-to-lead rate sits well below the Search channel average.
4. Keep the QA pixel test out of client reporting; it adds noise and nothing else.
5. Re-run this audit in a month and compare against the baselines above.

Method notes: all figures come from the export's rows only. CPL is total spend divided by total leads for the group named, not an average of daily CPLs. Click-to-lead rate is leads divided by clicks.
